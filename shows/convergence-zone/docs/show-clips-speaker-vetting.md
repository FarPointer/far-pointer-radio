# Speaker vetting for candidate show clips

**Date:** 2026-08-08 · **Branch:** `copilot/collect-cool-radio-clips`

Due diligence on every person considered for a Convergence Zone spoken-word drop, so the
decision to air — or not air — someone's voice is auditable later without re-deriving it.

Companion document: [`show-clips-candidates.md`](show-clips-candidates.md) — the clips
themselves, with the FCC screen.

## Scope and method

Each person was checked for credible, publicly reported evidence of sexual abuse, assault, or
harassment; relationships with minors; domestic violence; racism or antisemitism; extremist
political affiliation; or other conduct that would create reputational risk for a nonprofit
community station airing their voice.

Findings are drawn from major newspapers, wire services, court records, and subjects' own
statements. Rumour and unsourced claims were excluded. Allegation, admission, conviction, and
investigated-and-cleared are kept distinct throughout, because they are not the same thing and
collapsing them is how this kind of list goes wrong.

**This is a due-diligence summary, not a legal opinion, and CLEAR means "no credible reports
found in the scope of this research" — not a guarantee.** Verdicts should be re-checked for
living subjects before any long-lived use.

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CLEAR** | No credible reports found |
| **CAUTION** | Something a decision-maker should know before choosing |
| **FLAG** | Substantiated or admitted conduct creating significant reputational risk |

## Flagged

**John Peel** (BBC DJ, 1939–2004). Married Shirley Ann Milburn in Texas in 1965, when she was
15 and he was 25; he later said he had not known her age. In a 1989 *Radio Times* interview he
described teenage fans as wanting him to abuse them sexually, "which of course I was only too
happy to do" — his own words, not an allegation. Jane Nevin has publicly stated she became
pregnant by Peel during a relationship that began when she was 15, in 1969. No charges were
ever brought; Peel died in 2004. Glastonbury renamed its John Peel Stage in 2023 after public
pressure. The clearest case on this list.
[Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/voices/john-peel-glastonbury-stage-petition-rename-b2116331.html) ·
[Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/2023/03/05/glastonbury-renames-john-peel-stage-amid-underage-girls-controversy/) ·
[IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/john-peel-pregnant-girl-15-jane-nevin-393577)

**David Bowie** (1947–2016). Lori Maddox (b. 1958) has said in multiple interviews, including
with *Thrillist* in 2015, that she first had sex with Bowie at 15, around 1974 — conduct that
was statutory rape under California law. No charges were ever brought; Bowie never addressed
it publicly and his estate has not responded. **Allegation, not a finding.** Named, specific,
repeatedly given by the person herself, and involving a minor.
[Wikipedia — Lori Mattix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lori_Mattix)

**Guglielmo Marconi** (1874–1937). Joined the National Fascist Party in 1923 and remained close
to Mussolini until his death. President of the Royal Academy of Italy and a member of the
Grand Council of Fascism; publicly defended the invasion of Ethiopia. Credible accounts
describe his participation in excluding Jewish candidates from the Academy, their files marked
"e" for *ebreo*. Documented history, not allegation; not in scholarly dispute.
[WorldCrunch](https://worldcrunch.com/tech-science/guglielmo-marconi-wireless-inventor/) ·
[Italic Institute](https://italic.org/master-of-the-ether/)

**Karlheinz Stockhausen** (1928–2007). At a Hamburg press conference on 2001-09-16, five days
after the attacks, he described the destruction of the World Trade Center as "the greatest
work of art that exists for the whole cosmos." Festival concerts were cancelled. He later said
he had been misunderstood and was speaking of Lucifer within his own compositional mythology;
the clarification was widely rejected. No personal misconduct — a documented public statement
about a mass-casualty attack.
[NY Mag 9/11 Encyclopedia](https://images.nymag.com/news/9-11/10th-anniversary/karlheinz-stockhausen/) ·
[PMLA / Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/pmla/article/911-as-avantgarde-art/9231A28C5A6B77818F28FD44C1BAC782)

**Alan Freed** (1921–1965). Pleaded guilty in December 1962 to two counts of commercial bribery
for taking payments in exchange for airplay, receiving a $300 fine and a suspended sentence. A
conviction, not an allegation. Not a personal-conduct matter, and payola is contextually
understood as an industry-wide practice of its era — but it is a criminal conviction for
selling airtime, which lands differently on a listener-supported station.
[Wikipedia — Payola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payola)

**Kurt Waldheim** (1918–2007) — relevant only as a voice *on* the Voyager Golden Record, where
he recorded a message as UN Secretary-General in 1977. His concealed service as a Wehrmacht
intelligence officer in a unit involved in Balkan atrocities emerged during his 1986 Austrian
presidential campaign; he was subsequently barred from entering the United States. Use any
other part of the record.

## Caution

**Neil deGrasse Tyson** (b. 1958). In late 2018 four women made public allegations of sexual
misconduct, including a rape allegation from the 1980s by Tchiya Amet and separate accounts of
unwanted touching and advances. Fox, National Geographic, and the American Museum of Natural
History each investigated independently and cleared him in early 2019; he returned to his
on-air roles. No charges were filed. He denied all allegations. Formally cleared — and the
controversy is still in public memory, which is the part that matters for a drop.
[BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/azeenghorayshi/neil-degrasse-tyson-sexual-allegations-four-women) ·
[CBS New York](https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/neil-degrasse-tyson-cleared-nyc/)

**Hunter S. Thompson** (1937–2005). In 1990 producer Gail Palmer-Slater alleged Thompson
sexually assaulted her at his Colorado home; he was charged with sexual assault and drug
offences after a police raid. The charges were **dismissed** by the court for insufficient
evidence, with criticism of the prosecution's handling. Thompson consistently denied the
allegations. No other credible allegations are on record.
[UPI archive](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1990/05/22/Porno-movie-producer-testifies-against-Hunter-Thompson/3453643348800/)

**Brian Eno** (b. 1948). No allegations of personal misconduct of any kind. The only
controversy is political: he is a vocal BDS supporter, and advocacy groups including CAMERA UK
have argued his public statements evoke antisemitic tropes — a charge he denies, and one no
neutral body has found against him. A contested political dispute, recorded here only so the
station is not surprised by it.
[CAMERA UK](https://camera-uk.org/2021/02/07/guardian-op-ed-by-brian-eno-evokes-antisemitic-tropes-whilst-denying-bds-is-antisemitic/)

**Alan Watts** (1915–1973). Severe alcoholism, documented in his own autobiography and by his
biographers and his daughter, and cited as contributing to his death at 58; multiple marriages
and acknowledged affairs. **No credible allegations of sexual misconduct, abuse, or
harassment** in any biography or mainstream source. The only question is whether the station
wants to sit with the distance between the teaching and the life.

**Casey Kasem** (1932–2014). Nothing is alleged against Kasem himself. After his death his
children sued his widow, Jean Kasem, for wrongful death and elder abuse; prosecutors in
California and Washington investigated and declined to charge, and the civil suit settled in
2019 with no finding of liability. Included because the dispute is well known and attaches to
his name — the conduct at issue is not his. His unusable material is a language problem, not a
conduct one; see the FCC screen.
[NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/celebrity/casey-kasem-s-family-cleared-wrongdoing-investigation-his-death-n942216)

## Open questions

1. **A house rule for CAUTION speakers.** Tyson and Watts need one now; the next name will
   need the same answer. Better to decide the rule than to re-argue each case.
2. **Whether the flags are absolute.** Recommendation is that they are. If one is overridden,
   the reason belongs in this file, next to the finding it overrides.
3. **Re-checking living subjects.** A verdict on a living person has a shelf life. Anything
   that goes into rotation should be re-checked before it runs for a long stretch.
