**<u>Mic Break 1</u>**

You're tuned into Convergence Zone - a journey through the ambient soundscapes of the Pacific Northwest, along with artists from around the world that capture the spirit of the places we call home. Our region boasts an amazing array of artists and labels who create ambient, new age, electronic, experimental, dream-pop, neo-classical, and so much more. On Convergence Zone we celebrate their work -- and we also feature the legendary and contemporary artists who inspire them. I'm your host, Jim Causey.

You just heard "Index" by Norm Chambers - off the soundtrack to the 2017 documentary "Graphical Means". Tonight on Convergence Zone we'll hear pieces from several soundtracks, including performances by Wendy Carlos, Deep Forest, and Brian Eno. I'll also play new music from NAAVI, and the latest from the legendary Seattle experimental group Climax golden twins.  

But let get started with Suzanne CHANI -- a pioneer of electronic sound design and new age music, and perhaps the greatest living master of the BOOKLA synthesizer. Let's listen to "The Eighth Wave" from her 1986 album "The velocity of love" - on Convergence Zone, 90.7 KSER Everett, 89.9 KXIR Freeland.
