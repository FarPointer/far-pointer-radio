Women, non-binary, femme, and trans musicians have played vital roles in atmospheric music throughout history — as pioneers in style and form, technical and engineering leaders, and performers across instrumental and genre boundaries

This week on Convergence Zone, we celebrate these roles in honor of Women’s History Month in the US & UK:

Tributes to Delia Derbyshire, Wendy Carlos, and Suzanne Ciani (on tour now)

Local music from Mt Fog, Midday Veil, Chromatics, Grouper, and Rebecca Wolf

New music from Rachel Therrien and NAVVI; recent music from Caterina Barbieri, Julianna Barwick with Mary Lattimore, L’Rain, and many more surprises

Convergence Zone arrives Tuesdays at 10:30pm PDT on 90.7 KSER-FM, 89.9 KXIR-FM, also streaming live at kser.org and other internet radio sources

Women, trans, and non-binary artists have always been at the forefront of a contemplative music - pioneers and innovators in instruments and technology, forms and styles, and creative brilliance across space music, ambient, dream-pop, free jazz -- and beyond. Tonight on Convergence Zone we celebrate the work of femme, female, and non-gender-confirming musicians from our region and beyond.  

You just heard Mt Fog -- Seattle-based project of Carolyn B, with the Graduation remix of her song "Blue Summer". We opened the show with "Cherry" by Chromatics, from their 2016 album of the same name on Italians Do It Better.  

Later on in the show we'll have more local music from Midday Veil, Patricia Wolf, and Grouper. We'll also feature tributes to pioneering electronic musicians Delia Derbyshire, Suzanne Ciani, and Wendy Carlos.

But now let's get things started with Caroline Polachek -- her 2019 song "Door" -- on Independent Public Radio, KSER.
