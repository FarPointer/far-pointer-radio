**<u>Convergence Zone</u>**: a journey through the ambient soundscapes of the Pacific Northwest, along with artists from around the world that capture the spirit of the places we call home.  

on this week’s Convergence Zone, tons of new music from Stellarium, style.king.of.the.week, Rudy Adrian, Siren of the Sea, and NAVVI

music for solidarity and reproductive rights with songs by Kristen Roos and Loscil from Hotham Sound’s compilation “Calliope”, raising proceeds for NARAL Pro Choice America

classics by {arsonist}, Steve Roach, Herbie Hancock, and Colin Chin — and some surprises too

the Convergence Zone arrives 10:30pm PDT, Tuesday, April 11 on 90.7 KSER-FM, 89.9 KXIR-FM, and streaming around the world at kser.org - and on TuneIn and other Internet radio
