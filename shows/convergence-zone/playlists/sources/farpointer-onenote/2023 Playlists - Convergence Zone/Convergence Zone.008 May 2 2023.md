Swap in some Loscil from new album
Maybe some Nightnoise
Some Japanese New Age?

spring continues to tease us in the Pacific Northwest with breakthrough days of sun and warmth — before returning to the damp grey that nurtures the moss

as the days get longer, we are seeing contemplative artists embark on tour — celebrating the legacy of New Age, ambient, and free jazz with concerts in our region and around the United States

on this week’s Convergence Zone, songs from the acts touring this spring — like synth pioneer Suzanne Ciani, New Age polymath David Arkenstone, and experimental wizard Arushi Jain

more touring artists — Colloboh with Qu’ran Shaheed, legends like Suso Saiz and Bitchin Bajas, LA acts Classixx and Matthewdavid, and neo-jazz icons Gogo Penguin

also - brand new music from Vancouver’s Noble Oak, Italy’s Jarguna and Seattle cozywave artist Hello Meteor, and a 2021 live excerpt from Howard Givens, Madhavi Devi, and Craig Padilla

Convergence Zone arrives Tuesdays at 10:30pm PDT on 90.7 KSER-FM, 89.9 KXIR-FM, and streaming at kser.org, T uneIn, and all fine internet radio services

📸: @sevwave / @ageofreflections, David Arkenstone, Colloboh, Qu’ran Shaheed, Bitchin Bajas, Projekt Records, Hello Meteor
