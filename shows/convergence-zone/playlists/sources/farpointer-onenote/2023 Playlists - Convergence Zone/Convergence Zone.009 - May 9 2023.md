Convergence Zone.009 - May 9 2023
Saturday, May 6, 2023
2:23 PM
The dark palettes of the Pacific Northwest evoke vibes of gothic music and synthwave for many area artists, and this week on Convergence Zone we embrace that vibe

Brand new music from Frore and Locsil with Lawrence English; recent tracks by Bart Hawkins and Remote Vision

Vancouver BC cityscapes from FM Attack and LEATHERS

Gothic classics from A Covenant of Thorns, New Order, Sisters of Mercy, and Depeche Mode

With more surprises in store

Convergence Zone arrives Tuesday night at 10:30 pm PDT on 90.7 KSER-FM, 89.9 KXIR-FM and streaming at kser.org, TuneIn, smart speakers - you get the drill
