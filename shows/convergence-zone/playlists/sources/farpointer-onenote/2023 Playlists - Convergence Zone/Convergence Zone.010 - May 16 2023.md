Convergence Zone.010 - May 16 2023
Saturday, May 6, 2023
2:23 PM
Live music
With more surprises in store

Convergence Zone arrives Tuesday night at 10:30 pm PDT on 90.7 KSER-FM, 89.9 KXIR-FM and streaming at kser.org, TuneIn, smart speakers - you get the drill
---
Celebrating the 10th episode of Convergence Zone with a show full of live recordings by atmospheric artists

Rare tracks from Dead Can Dance live in Seattle, Steve Roach in Philadelphia in 1986, Suzanne Ciani in Switzerland, and Cocteau Twins live on BBC Radio in 1996

Live classics from Tangerine Dream, Rudy Adrian, Redshift, Peter Gabriel, and Node

Convergence Zone arrives Tuesdays at 10:30pm PDT on 90.7 KSER FM in Everett, 89.9 KXIR in Freeland, and streaming at kser.org, TuneIn, and other radio services
