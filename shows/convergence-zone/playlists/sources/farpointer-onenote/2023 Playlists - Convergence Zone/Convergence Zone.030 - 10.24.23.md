Convergence Zone.030 - 10.24.23
Monday, October 23, 2023
8:40 PM

This week on Convergence Zone I’m thrilled to share the worldwide radio premiere of the new LP “Apeiron” by Terminus Void — and excerpts from my exclusive interview with project creator J. Ronald Smith. We spoke about his influences, the themes captured on this debut record on Groove Unlimited, and the story threaded through the musical layers on “Apeiron”

I’ve also got brand new local music from AeTopus and from Pure Bathing Culture, as well as by Wild Nothing, Laurel Halo, and Driftmachine; I'm also celebrating local tour stops by Eydis Evensen and Loscil with Lawrence English

I’ll also pay tribute to Norm Chambers, the prolific Seattle composer, performer, and music curator

I'll have some other autumn surprises too -- when Convergence Zone arrives on Tuesday night, 10:30pm Pacific time, 90.7 KSER and 89.9 KXIR -- streaming around the world at kser.org, TuneIn, and other fine radio services
