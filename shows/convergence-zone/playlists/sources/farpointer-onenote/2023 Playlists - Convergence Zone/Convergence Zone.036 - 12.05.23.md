The moods of winter in the Pacific Northwest tend toward gloom and damp - as days turn progressively shorter, with mist and rain joining ever-longer dark nights. These combinations can make winters heavy on the soul, but also bring renewal and the promise of abundant green life for the year to come. Artists from the Pacific Northwest often infuse their work with these shifting moods.
