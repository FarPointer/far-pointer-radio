Episode 062 - 07.23.24 - video games pledge drive
Monday, July 22, 2024
2:43 PM

Tomorrow night, for the KSER Summer Membership Drive, I'll present a special episode of Convergence Zone exploring the ambient soundscapes of video game soundtracks

As the creative scope of games have expanded across genre boundaries, the music accompanying them has also pushed creative boundaries - exploring ambient, contemplative, space music, and much more

Join me on a journey through game soundtracks by composers from the Pacific Northwest and beyond - and help power KSER by renewing your membership

I'll have video game scores by regional artists like Japanese Breakfast, Jon Everist, Lena Raine, Spencer Doran (Visible Cloaks), and Symbion Project - along with gaming soundtracks by 65daysofstatus, Tangerine Dream, Roxy Music, Tim Shiel, Hrishikesh Hirway, Future Sound of London, Austin Wintory, and much more  

Soundtracks spanning indie and blockbuster games and nearly 40 years - including Homeworld, Kentucky Route Zero, Sable, Nidhogg, Journey, Mass Effect, No Man's Sky, The 7th Guest, Celeste, Katamari Damacy, and much more

Convergence Zone celebrates ambient video game soundtracks for the KSER Summer Membership Drive - Tuesday, July 23 at 10:30pm PDT on 90.7 KSER, 89.9 KXIR, streaming around the world at kser.org
