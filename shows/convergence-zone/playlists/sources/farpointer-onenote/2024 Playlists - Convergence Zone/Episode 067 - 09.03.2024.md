New music from David Arkenstone, the 2024 album "Quest for the Runestone" - inspired by his love of Celtic, Nordic, and cinematic music traditions, reminiscent of his earlier fantasy "concept" albums - based on a story by Dashiell Han Arkenstone

In this half hour, brand-new music from Isostatic, and a live performance by Steve Roach with a song from "Dreamtime Return" - there's a new remaster of that album coming out in September 2024 -
But now let's go back to May, 1975, when Iasos released his debut album, "Interdimensional music" - one fo the very first records in the New Age genre. This pioneering artist was born in Greece, relocated to Berkeley Califfornia in the late 1960s - he said around that time he started hearing music in his head, that he believed was transmitted directly to him by a being on another plane of existence. Iasos died in January 2024 at the age of 76. From his 1975 debut album, This song is called Rainbow Canyon

---------------

Brand new music from England - Isostatic, aka Sean Costello - his latest album on Exosphere Records is a cinematic ambient journey, exploring one of nature’s lesser-known wonders: the short-lived electrical discharges that occur in the earth’s upper atmosphere during thunderstorms, collectively referred to as Transient Luminous Events. That song - the radio edit of "Red Sprites"

In 1988, Steve Roach released his two-CD magnum opus titled "Dreamtime Return" - inspired by his travels in the Australian outback, his studies of the Aboriginal Dreamtime, and his desert walkabouts in California. The album received a high-definition remaster for its thirtieth anniversary by engineer Howard Givens - now Projekt Records has announced a new vinyl release based on that remaster.  

Steve has also performed pieces from this legendary album in live sets around the world - including this rendition performed in Los Angeles in 2023, the song is called "The Continent"
