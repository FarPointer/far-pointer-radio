06.17.2025 - Summer Fund Drive
Tuesday, June 17, 2025
1:13 PM

The KSER Summer Membership Drive is on! Tonight on Convergence Zone, I'll be joined live in the booth by Michael Svob, long-time KSER supporter and new co-host for Convergence Zone. You may have already heard him on the air - he's hosting the show on alternating weeks, and I'm thrilled to have his musical taste and on-air presence helping make this program vibrant week after week.

We're celebrating the 2025 Cascadia NW Arts and Music Festival, taking place this weekend at Darrington Music Park. The event runs from Friday, June 20 at 1pm until Sunday, June 22 and 8pm in Darrington, WA - with live music, vinyl DJ sets, camping, and sessions on medication, community building, and non-violent communication.

We'll include music from some artists who will perform at the festival, including Seattle's own Future Spells, along with tunes by Enigma Beats, A Hundred Drums, Seeded Vision, and Sappho.

We'll be asking for your support to help make Convergence Zone and all the other KSER programming possible! You can give now by going to <https://kser.org>, clicking on the Summer Membership Drive button - and don't forget to mention Convergence Zone in your comments.
Convergence Zone - now at 8:30pm PT on 90.7 KSER-FM, 89.9 KXIR-FM, and streaming live around the world at <https://kser.org>.
