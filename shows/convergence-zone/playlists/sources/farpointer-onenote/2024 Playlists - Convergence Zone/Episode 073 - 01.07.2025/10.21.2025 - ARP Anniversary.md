This week on Convergence Zone, I'm preparing a special program for our 2025 Fall Fund Drive - celebrating the history of ARP Instruments.

This year marks the 100th anniversary of the birth of Alan R. Pearlman - founder of ARP Instruments. ARP's synthesizers play a crucial role throughout the history of electronic music; iconic instruments like the ARP 2600, the Odyssey, and the Solina String Ensemble can be heard in every genre of music from the 1970s through today.  

On Tuesday, October 21st, I'll ask for your financial support to power KSER and keep shows like Convergency Zone on the air. Every song will feature ARP synthesizers! I'll play artists you'd expect to hear on Convergence Zone, like Jean-Michel Jarre, Lisa Bella Donna, Steve Roach and Éliane Radigue - along with music from genres ranging from synth-pop, fusion, and funk from through 1970s through today. I'll try to work in every avatar of Vince Clarke too!  

Join me and my friend and co-host Michael G on Tuesday, October 21st at 8:30pm - you can call in your pledge or submit it online, and we'll take a tour through the history of ARP Instruments together.

Tuesday, October 21st, 8:30pm-10:30pm - 90.7 KSER, 89.9 KXIR, streaming around the world at kser.org, or on smart speakers or TuneIn.

Norman Ray Chambers Jr. passed away at home surrounded by his family on Sunday, Oct. 30, 2022 in Seattle, WA at age 50.
