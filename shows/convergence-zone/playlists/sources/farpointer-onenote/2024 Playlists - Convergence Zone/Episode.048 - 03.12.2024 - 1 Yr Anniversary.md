One Year: International Women’s Month

---
This week marks the one-year anniversary of Convergence Zone’s launch on KSER. I’m celebrating with a special program honoring International Women’s Month — ambient, new age, experimental, and space music by women and femme artists from around the world

Regional selections including Grouper, Jennifer Butler, Lena Raine, Raica, Saloli, and Domenica Diavolera

Legendary figures like Wendy Carlos, Beverly Glenn-Copeland, Pauline Anna Strom, Suzanne Ciani, Lisa Bella Donna, and CoWomennstance Demby

Music from Marine Eyes + ISKRE, Faten Kanaan, Mary Lattimore and Julianna Barwick, and Sign Libra

Convergence Zone celebrates its one year anniversary - Tuesday night at 10:30pm PDT on 90.7 KSER, 89.9 KXIR, and streaming at kser.org

You're tuned to 90.7 FM - KSER, Everett - and 89.9, KXIR, Freeland - and  

You've entered the Convergence Zone - a two-hour journey through the musical atmospheres of the PNW - and beyond. I'm your host, Jim Causey.  

Women and femme musicians have played a vital role in the creation of electronic and contemplative music throughout history - developing many technical breakthroughs for instruments and production systems - as well as defining the cutting edge for musical styles and genres. On this edition of Convergence Zone we recognize International Women's Day, and Women's History Month, by work of female artists from the Pacific Northwest and around the world.  

The show opened up with Seattle resident Nancy Rumbel, with "Tree of Life", from her 1995 album "Notes From the Tree Of Life". I'll also have music from legendary figures like Suzanne Ciani, Constance Demby, Wendy Carlos, Lisa Bella Donna - and regional works from Patricia Wolf, Domenica DE-a-voh-LER-ah, Raica, and Ann Annie.  

Right now, we're listening to Faten Kanaan - she grew up between Europe and the Middle East, currently based in New York - in 2024, she opened for Alessandro Cortini as part of the Reflections concert series. From here 2023 album "Afterpoem", this is "Trenchcoat" here on Convergence Zone.
